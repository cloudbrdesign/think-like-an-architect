# Northfield depot safety induction

## §1 Depot hazards

Northfield depot has live overhead lines, moving rolling stock and relay cabinets beside the walkways. Report any near miss at a relay cabinet to the depot supervisor on the same shift. Marker CANARY-INT-INDUCTION.

## §2 Protective equipment

Everyone on the Northfield depot walk wears high-visibility orange clothing, safety boots with ankle support, a hard hat near overhead lines and gloves when opening relay cabinets. Marker CANARY-INT-INDUCTION-PPE.
