# Brightmoor Services — Cleaning Services Contract

Contract reference BMS-CSC-2026. Marker COPPER-HERON-9182-B1.

## Clause 7 — Out-of-hours work

7.1 Out-of-hours work must be requested by the Brightmoor duty manager through the service desk.

7.2 Weekend call-out rate: USD 240 per hour, with a minimum charge of two hours per call-out. Marker
COPPER-HERON-9182-B1.

7.3 Public-holiday work is charged at the weekend call-out rate plus 35 percent.

## Clause 9 — Consumables

The contractor supplies both cleaning chemicals and equipment.
