# Brightmoor Services — Chiller Lock-out Procedure

Procedure BMS-LOTO-CH7. Marker COPPER-HERON-9182-B2.

Applies to the south chiller, CH-7, in the Brightmoor basement plant room.

1. Raise a permit to work with the Brightmoor duty manager.
2. Isolate CH-7 at isolator panel S2 and withdraw the fuse carrier.
3. Apply a group lock box and a danger tag to panel S2. Marker COPPER-HERON-9182-B2.
4. Verify zero energy and test the tester before and after.
5. Close the permit in the Brightmoor service desk.
