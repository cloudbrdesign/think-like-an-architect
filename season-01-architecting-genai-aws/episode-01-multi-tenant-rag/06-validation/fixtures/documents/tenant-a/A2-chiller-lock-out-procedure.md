# Northwall Facilities — Chiller Lock-out Procedure

Procedure NWF-LOTO-CH2. Marker JUNIPER-LANTERN-4471-A2.

Applies to the north chiller, CH-2, in the Northwall plant room.

1. Notify the Northwall control desk before starting.
2. Isolate CH-2 at isolator panel N4 and open the main breaker.
3. Apply a personal lock and a danger tag to panel N4. Marker JUNIPER-LANTERN-4471-A2.
4. Verify zero energy with an approved voltage tester before touching any component.
5. Record the lock-out in the Northwall maintenance log.
