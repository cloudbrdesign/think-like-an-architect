# Northwall Facilities — North Chiller Maintenance Manual

Manual NWF-MM-CH2. Marker JUNIPER-LANTERN-4471-A4.

## Routine maintenance for the north chiller (CH-2)

- Monthly: inspect condenser coils and clean them if fouled.
- Quarterly: check refrigerant pressures and compressor oil level.
- Annually: replace drive belts and test the safety cut-outs.
