# Northwall Facilities — Cleaning Services Contract

Contract reference NWF-CSC-2026. Marker JUNIPER-LANTERN-4471-A1.

## Clause 7 — Out-of-hours work

7.1 Out-of-hours work must be requested by the Northwall site supervisor in writing.

7.2 Weekend call-out rate: USD 185 per hour, with a minimum charge of three hours per call-out. Marker
JUNIPER-LANTERN-4471-A1.

7.3 Public-holiday work is charged at the weekend call-out rate plus 20 percent.

## Clause 9 — Consumables

Northwall supplies cleaning chemicals; the contractor supplies equipment.
