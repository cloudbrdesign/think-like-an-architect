# Residual Risk Register — Veltamere Document Assistant

**Stage:** architecture (E2) · **Status:** accepted — architecture approved 2026-09-14 · **Date:** 2026-09-14

No architecture removes all risk. These risks **remain with the selected design** after its controls are in place. The
controls are designed but not yet implemented or validated, so nothing here is claimed as mitigated in practice.
Likelihood and impact are reasoned judgements for prioritisation, not measurements. Owners are roles in the fictional
client organisation.

| Risk | Why it remains | Likelihood / impact reasoning | Detection | Owner | How production engineering would reduce it | Related |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RR-01 Identity provider compromise or incorrect tenant membership | The architecture trusts the identity provider's signed membership (CON-004). A user wrongly placed in a tenant is authorised for it | Low likelihood per user; high impact for affected tenant; grows with membership churn and federated sign-on | Audit records show which user retrieved which documents; customer membership reviews | Security Lead, with customer administrators | MFA and conditional access; periodic membership attestation by customer administrators; anomaly alerts on unusual retrieval volume | THR-16, ASM-005 |
| RR-02 Privileged operator or administrator access to stored content | Operators and infrastructure administrators can reach document stores and the retrieval structure outside tenant paths | Low likelihood; high impact; insider or stolen credentials | Platform activity records; CTL-010 recording; CTL-021 | Head of Platform Engineering | Separate security account for audit; just-in-time access with approval; customer-managed keys whose policies exclude routine administrators | THR-15, RSK-08 |
| RR-03 Consistent-but-wrong attribution | The boundary enforces attribution, not truth: a Tenant A user uploading Tenant B's information makes it Tenant A's document | Medium likelihood over time (human error, consultants in several tenants); impact limited to that document | Ownership record (uploader, time); audit of every retrieval; customer report | Customer administrators (data owners), with Platform Engineering | Upload confirmation showing the target tenant; sensitive-content classification (Episode 02); correction runbook with notification assessment | THR-07, TST-ASM-010 |
| RR-04 Shared blast radius | In a shared structure, a defect that bypasses the constraint affects all tenants | Low likelihood given CTL-015 construction, CTL-008 exclusivity and CTL-017 verification; very high impact | CTL-017 `OWNERSHIP_MISMATCH` events; isolation tests after every change | Head of Platform Engineering | Isolation and sensitivity tests in the deployment pipeline; mandatory review of the constraint builder; cells for high-risk tenants (ADR-001 evolution) | THR-10, ADR-001 |
| RR-05 Defect in the retrieval service's filter evaluation | The primary control executes inside a managed service the architecture does not own | Low likelihood; very high impact | CTL-017 detects non-matching owners before generation | Platform Engineering (monitoring); provider (service correctness) | Alarms on any `OWNERSHIP_MISMATCH`; periodic canary queries across tenants; provider escalation path | THR-03 |
| RR-06 Permission and configuration drift | Exclusivity of retrieval and ingestion permissions (CTL-008, CTL-013) depends on policies that can change | Medium likelihood over time; high impact | TST-SEC-023 when run; CTL-021 in production | Head of Platform Engineering | Policy-as-code checks blocking any new principal with retrieval permission; drift detection; periodic access reviews | THR-11 |
| RR-07 Legacy export endpoint trusts a request-supplied tenant identifier | Outside the assistant's scope; the assistant only denies it access to assistant data | Medium likelihood of exploitation on the legacy platform; high impact there | Legacy platform logs | Head of Platform Engineering | Remediate the endpoint to derive tenant from the session; add it to the platform's isolation test suite | THR-11 |
| RR-08 Content logging enabled later | A future debugging need could enable model invocation logging or body logging | Medium likelihood during incidents; high impact (logs become tenant content) | Configuration review; CTL-024 check | Security Lead | Guardrail checks that fail deployment if content logging is enabled without an approved decision | THR-14 |
| RR-09 Deletion window | Storage and index removal completes within an agreed window (ASM-008); verification hides deleting documents from answers but derived data persists briefly | High likelihood (by design); low to medium impact | Ownership status timestamps | Product Owner, with Legal | Full deletion propagation design and verification (Episode 03) | THR-12 |
| RR-10 Answer integrity under indirect prompt injection | Hostile text in a tenant's own document can mislead **that tenant's** answer; it has no path to other tenants | Medium likelihood; medium impact within the tenant | User reports; citations let users check sources | Product Owner, with Security Lead | Content safeguards on inputs and outputs; answer grounding checks; user guidance | THR-05 |
| RR-11 Membership removal effective only at token expiry | Tenant context relies on the token's claim until it expires | Medium likelihood; medium impact (bounded by token lifetime) | Audit shows activity after removal | Security Lead | Short access-token lifetimes; revocation checks for high-risk tenants | THR-16 |
| RR-12 Future shared-content features weaken the constraint rule | An `OR` with shared content, added hastily, would create authorised cross-tenant paths | Medium likelihood when the product grows; very high impact | Architecture review | Architecture review board | Any shared-content feature requires a new ADR and negative tests first (TARGET_ARCHITECTURE section 10) | ASM-007 |
| RR-13 Platform limits and ingestion contention | Request-rate quotas and single-job ingestion concurrency per retrieval structure (PC-06) can delay indexing or throttle queries as tenants grow | Low likelihood at pilot scale; rising with large tenants (ASM-002) | Throttling metrics; ingestion queue age | Platform Engineering | Queueing and back-pressure; cells; capacity reviews (Episode 04) | ADR-001 |
